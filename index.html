Turn your LinkedIn, Instagram or Viva Engage stats into an easy-to-read PDF.

1

Your analytics

Leave blank anything you don't have.
How do I get my analytics file?

LinkedIn post: open the post, tap View analytics, then Export.

LinkedIn profile or page: go to your Analytics (or the page's Analytics tab) and press Export.

Instagram / Facebook: in Meta Business Suite go to Insights and use Export data. The Instagram app itself has no export, so type the numbers in instead.

Viva Engage community: open the community's Analytics dashboard and press Export to get a .csv.

Any other spreadsheet of social stats works too. The tool looks for things like impressions, reach, likes, comments, shares and dates.
